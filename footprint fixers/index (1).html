⚖️ Before vs After

❌ Before: Risky habits

  • Live location visible in posts.
  • Birthday and college ID are public.
  • A Wi-Fi password screenshot is posted.
  • The same password is reused.

✅ After: Safer habits

  • Disable unnecessary location tagging.
  • Hide personal identifiers from public bio.
  • Never share passwords or screenshots containing secrets.
  • Use unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.
  • Review and remove unneeded third-party app access.

🛡️ Quick Privacy Tips

  1. Use fictional or anonymised data in demonstrations.
  2. Think about who can view a post before publishing.
  3. Avoid sharing travel plans or location in real time.
  4. Review privacy settings, tagged posts and app permissions regularly.
  5. This educational checklist is not a professional security audit.
